PROJECT

Athena

Athena is a personal, self-hosted productivity system built by Eddie Xiao to manage his own calendar, tasks, email, and notes in one place.

What it does

  • Capture and organize: sorts quick notes and ideas into tasks, calendar events, and a personal knowledge base.
  • Calendar and tasks: reads a designated Google Calendar and Google Tasks inbox, and creates or updates events and tasks, such as deadlines, flights, and bill due dates.
  • Email triage: reads Gmail (read-only) to summarize and categorize messages, and files receipts, bills, and tickets into a private document archive.
  • Search: one search across notes and documents.

Athena is used only by its developer. It is not a public service and has no sign-up.

How it uses Google data

Athena uses Gmail (read-only), Google Calendar, and Google Tasks only to provide the features above, for the account owner. Google data is never sold, never used for advertising, never sent to third-party commercial AI providers, and never used to train AI models. See the full details in the policy below.
